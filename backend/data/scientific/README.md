# Modern Scientific Evidence Corpus

Store peer-reviewed biomedical, clinical, and pharmacological literature validating or scrutinizing Ayurvedic treatments and herbal pharmacology:

- **PubMed & MEDLINE indexed articles** (clinical trials, systematic reviews, meta-analyses)
- **AYUSH Research Portal publications**
- **Pharmacological evaluations** (phytochemistry, toxicity profiles, bioavailability studies)
- **Clinical trial PDFs and structured study extracts**
